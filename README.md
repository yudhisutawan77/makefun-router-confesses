Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Router Confesses"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-the-router-confesses-f98f6b9e): a single line (115 characters) that a home Wi-Fi router would say to excuse itself for dropping the connection at the exact moment a movie reaches its climax.

> I heard you bet twenty bucks on the wrong killer, so I cut the feed at the climax — your wallet can thank me later.

`main.py` writes that one line to the output file (`$ENTRY_OUT`, `$ENTRY_OUT_DIR/entry.txt`, or `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it to stdout.
